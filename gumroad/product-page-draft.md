## You can edit. Getting clients is the part nobody taught you.

**For beginner and early-stage freelance video editors.**

The edit is usually the part you've practised. What's often missing is everything
around it: what to offer, who to contact, what to say, when to follow up and how
to price a project.

CLIENT MACHINE turns that into one repeatable workflow, with a day-by-day plan
that tells you what to do next.

## Getting clients isn't one skill. It's a sequence.

Offer → Price → Proof → Prospects → Outreach → Follow-up → Conversation → Proposal → Onboarding → Delivery

It's easy to jump straight to cold DMs and skip the offer and the follow-up.
CLIENT MACHINE gives every step its own tool:

- **Offer:** Offer Builder. Decide who you help, what you deliver and what's not included.
- **Price:** Pricing System. Scope first, price second, with no made-up "market rates".
- **Proof:** Portfolio Builder. Choose or create relevant, honestly presented work.
- **Prospects:** 30-Day Client Acquisition Plan. One day, one action, one deliverable.
- **Outreach:** Outreach Script Library. Frameworks to adapt, not copy-paste spam.
- **Follow-up:** Prospect Tracker. See who's due today, who's overdue and who to re-engage.
- **Conversation:** Sales System. Turn interest into a clear next step, on a call or by message.
- **Proposal:** Proposal Kit, including a reusable proposal template.
- **Onboarding:** Client Onboarding Kit. Collect what the work needs and start properly.
- **Delivery:** Delivery & Retention. Send work, handle feedback and earn repeat work.

A Start Here Guide shows you how the pieces fit together.

## What it won't do

- It won't contact prospects for you or find them automatically.
- It can't promise clients or income. Results depend on your offer, portfolio, outreach and follow-up.
- It isn't a video-editing course. It covers the client side of freelancing.

Some modules include AI drafting prompts. AI writes a first draft; you check the
names, facts, prices and tone before anything is sent. No AI software is included.

## Who it's for

**A good fit if you:**
- can edit, but don't have a steady way to find clients
- send outreach in bursts, then stop
- aren't sure how to package or price your service
- want one process to follow rather than more scattered advice

**Not a fit if you:**
- want guaranteed clients or a done-for-you lead service
- want editing tutorials
- already run a mature sales process and need agency-level systems

## FAQ

**Is this a course?**
No. It's a set of workbooks, scripts, a 30-day plan and a tracker that you work through and fill in.

**What do I receive?**
One ZIP file with the 10 module PDFs, the Prospect Tracker (.xlsx) and a one-page PDF with your Canva template links. You can print the worksheets or fill them in on screen.

**What software do I need?**
A PDF reader and a spreadsheet app that opens .xlsx files. [[Name the apps you've tested.]] Canva (free account) is only needed for the two editable templates.

**Can I use the templates with my clients?**
Yes. The licence is for one buyer and your own freelance work. You can customise the proposal and onboarding templates and use them with your clients. You can't share, resell or redistribute CLIENT MACHINE or its templates.

**Will I get updates?**
You get the version available when you buy. We may add updates later.
