Getting clients isn't one skill. It's a sequence.

Offer → Price → Proof → Prospects → Outreach → Follow-up → Conversation → Proposal → Onboarding → Delivery

It's easy to jump straight to cold DMs and skip the offer and the follow-up. CLIENT MACHINE gives every step its own tool:

  • Offer: Offer Builder. Decide who you help, what you deliver and what's not included.
  • Price: Pricing System. Scope first, price second, with no made-up "market rates".
  • Proof: Portfolio Builder. Choose or create relevant, honestly presented work.
  • Prospects: 30-Day Client Acquisition Plan. One day, one action, one deliverable.
  • Outreach: Outreach Script Library. Frameworks to adapt, not copy-paste spam.
  • Follow-up: Prospect Tracker. See who's due today, who's overdue and who to re-engage.
  • Conversation: Sales System. Turn interest into a clear next step, on a call or by message.
  • Proposal: Proposal Kit, including a reusable proposal template.
  • Onboarding: Client Onboarding Kit. Collect what the work needs and start properly.
  • Delivery: Delivery & Retention. Send work, handle feedback and earn repeat work.

A Start Here Guide shows you how the pieces fit together.

What it won't do

  • It won't contact prospects for you or find them automatically.
  • It can't promise clients or income. Results depend on your offer, portfolio, outreach and follow-up.
  • It isn't a video-editing course. It covers the client side of freelancing.

Some modules include AI drafting prompts. AI writes a first draft; you check the names, facts, prices and tone before anything is sent. No AI software is included.

Who it's for

A good fit if you:

  • can edit, but don't have a steady way to find clients
  • send outreach in bursts, then stop
  • aren't sure how to package or price your service
  • want one process to follow rather than more scattered advice

Not a fit if you:

  • want guaranteed clients or a done-for-you lead service
  • want editing tutorials
  • already run a mature sales process and need agency-level systems
