## What was good
- **Ads delivered cleanly:** no rejections or delivery errors; CPM RM 33–39 for US traffic.
- **The Problem message clearly beat Demo** at getting clicks (about 2× the CTR, half the cost per visitor).
- **Problem 4:5 was the single best ad:** 3.1% CTR, RM 2.00 per landing page view.
- **People who clicked stayed:** 77% of clicks became landing page views, and 70% of those reached the product page, so the link, page load and pixel work.
- **Reels outperformed Feed** on CTR.
- **Retargeting audiences exist** (store visitors, checkout starters, Page engagers) and are filling up.

## What was bad
1. **Zero checkouts from 47 visitors.** If even 5% of visitors normally clicked Buy, seeing 0 in 47 would happen only about 9% of the time. That points to the **Gumroad page or offer** (price, proof, clarity), not the ads.
2. **Weak video hooks:** only 8–12% of impressions became 3-second views, average watch time was 1–2 s on 21–23 s videos, and about 1% watched through. Most people never saw the message or price.
3. **Vertical videos ran mostly in Feed** (94% of spend), where they lost to the 4:5 version and where Reels did better.
4. **The budget was too thin for learning:** 3 ad sets × RM 6.66 split the data three ways.
5. **No social proof** on the ads: 0 reactions or comments.
6. **Meta can't see sales,** so it can't optimise for buyers or report ROAS.

## What to improve
- **Gumroad page first:** clear headline, preview images of real pages, a short demo on the page, testimonials or beta-user quotes, a refund guarantee, and a mobile-first layout (all traffic was mobile).
- **Lower-commitment first step:** a free sample (for example, 5 outreach scripts) or a lower launch price to start sales and build an email list.
- **Hooks:** put the pain point on screen in the first second, add captions and a voiceover, and test 2–3 new openings for the Problem ad.
- **Format:** use 4:5 for Feed and 9:16 only where it fits (Reels and Stories).
- **Structure:** one campaign, one ad set, 2–3 Problem variants plus Workflow. Drop Demo from cold traffic; use it for retargeting.
- **Measurement:** confirm Purchase in Meta with one test sale before optimising for sales.

## Plan to reach first sales (proposal, nothing created)

**Phase 0: fix the funnel before spending (no ad spend).**
1. Review and upgrade the Gumroad page (see above). Share screenshots, or allow `lumigoods.gumroad.com` in this environment so Claude can review it.
2. Add a free lead magnet on Gumroad ($0 product, e.g. "5 cold DM scripts for video editors") that links to the $47 product. Optionally test a launch price (e.g. $27 for the first 50 buyers).
3. Verify the Purchase event in Meta with one real test purchase, so the next campaign can optimise for and report sales.

**Phase 1: cold traffic, 7 days, about RM 20/day, one ad set.**
- Ads: 3 Problem hook variants (4:5 Feed, 9:16 Reels and Stories) plus Workflow 4:5.
- If a lead magnet exists: send traffic to it and optimise for leads or the free "purchase". Otherwise keep landing page views as the goal.
- Placements: Facebook Feed and Reels (add Instagram Feed and Reels once connected); no Audience Network.
- Audience: US, 25–44, the same interests. Test Advantage+ audience as a second ad set only after the page converts.

**Phase 2: retargeting (once an audience passes about 100 people).**
- Small ad set at RM 5/day targeting store visitors, checkout starters and Page engagers. Use the Demo video plus guarantee or limited-price copy. Exclude buyers once Purchase is tracked.

**Decision rules**

| After about 100 landing page views | Action |
|---|---|
| ≥ 1 checkout and ≥ 1 sale | Keep it; consider moving to a Sales objective. |
| Checkouts but no sales | Check price and checkout friction on Gumroad. |
| 0 checkouts again | Rework the offer before more ad spend. |

Stop any ad whose cost per landing page view stays above RM 5 after 1,000 impressions.
