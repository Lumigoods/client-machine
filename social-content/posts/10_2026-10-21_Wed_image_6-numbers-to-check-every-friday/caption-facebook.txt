"Outreach isn't working" is too vague to fix. Numbers make it fixable.

Every Friday, count six things:
Messages sent → Replies → Positive replies → Conversations → Proposals → Clients won

Then look for the biggest drop:
📉 Lots of messages, few replies → your first message or prospect list needs work
📉 Replies but few positive ones → the offer isn't clear or isn't relevant to them
📉 Conversations but no proposals → you're not suggesting a clear next step
📉 Proposals but no yes → check scope, price and how you present it

If you send a handful of messages a week, the numbers will be small, and that's fine. You're looking for patterns over several weeks, not perfect stats.
