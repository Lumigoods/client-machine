Most beginner editors start with outreach. That's why it feels so hard. You're pitching before you know what you're pitching.

Give yourself one foundation week first:
Day 1: choose your target client
Day 2: choose your core service
Day 3: build your offer (one sentence)
Day 4: set your starting pricing
Day 5: audit your portfolio
Day 6: create or improve examples
Day 7: make your profile client-ready

Small tasks, one a day. By Day 8, your messages have something solid behind them.
